Fractional Knapsack with Deterioration Rate
An item with large decay rate loses value faster.
So, items with high deterioration should be handled earlier.

A useful greedy interpretation

At time t, calculate: the score(i) at t equal to v(i)/w(i) - lambda(i).t

Choose the item having maximum current score.

Then:

Take as much of it as possible.
Update remaining capacity.
Move to the next time.
Recalculate the effective densities.

PSEUDOCODE-
Fractional-Knapsack-With-Decay (items, W)
    for each item i:
        density[i]=value[i]/weight[i]

    time = 0
    totalValue = 0

    while W>0 and there are unused items:
        for every available item i:
            effective[i] = density[i] - lambda[i] * time

        choose item j with maximum effective[j]

        amount = min(weight[j], W)

        totalValue += amount * effective[j]

        W = W - amount

        mark j as processed
        time = time + 1

    return totalValue


COMPLEXITY-
Time- O(n^2)
Space- O(n)
