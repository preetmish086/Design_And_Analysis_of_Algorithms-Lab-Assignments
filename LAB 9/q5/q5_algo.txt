PSEUDOCODE-
for every child:
    left[i] = 1
    right[i] = 1

for i = 1 to n-1:
    if rating[i] > rating[i-1]:
        left[i] = left[i-1] + 1

for i = n-2 down to 0:
    if rating[i] > rating[i+1]:
        right[i] = right[i+1] + 1

total = 0

for i = 0 to n-1:
    candy = max(left[i], right[i])
    total += candy

return total

COMPLEXITY-
Time- O(n)
Space- O(n)
